# Issuing invoices correctly

Last verified: 2026-10-19. Source: Real Decreto 1619/2012 (Reglamento de facturación),
Real Decreto-ley 15/2025 (VeriFactu dates).

## Mandatory content of a full invoice
- Number (consecutive series, e.g. 2026-001, 2026-002 — no gaps) and issue date.
- Issuer: full name, NIF, address. Client: name/company, tax ID (EU VAT number for EU businesses), address.
- Description of the service, taxable base, VAT rate and amount (or the legal reason for no VAT).
- Date of the service if different from the issue date.

## Legal mention when no Spanish VAT is charged
- Exempt activity: "Operación exenta de IVA según el artículo 20.Uno.<n>º de la Ley 37/1992".
- B2B service to an EU business, not subject in Spain: "Inversión del sujeto pasivo —
  operación no sujeta según el artículo 69.Uno.1º de la Ley 37/1992" / "Reverse charge".
Which one applies is settled in the setup (see quarterly-filing `vat-foreign-clients.md`).

## IRPF withholding on invoices
Only invoices to **Spanish** businesses/professionals carry IRPF withholding (15%, or 7% in the
first three years). Invoices to clients abroad carry none.

## VeriFactu (invoicing software)
Under Real Decreto-ley 15/2025, the obligation for autónomos who invoice with software
starts on **1 July 2027** (companies: 1 January 2027). From then, invoicing software must be
VeriFactu-compliant. Whether generic tools (word processor, spreadsheet) count as invoicing
software is a grey area: check the AEAT VeriFactu FAQ, and re-check the date, before mid-2027.

## Keep
Every issued and received invoice for at least **4 years** (6 for the commercial books).
