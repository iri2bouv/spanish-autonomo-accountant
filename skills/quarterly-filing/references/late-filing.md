# Late filing — what it costs (art. 27 Ley General Tributaria)

Last verified: 2026-10-19.

When a return is filed late **on your own initiative** (before any AEAT request):
- surcharge of **1% + 1% per full month** of delay, on the amount due;
- after **12 months**: 15% + late-payment interest (intereses de demora) from the 13th month;
- **no penalty (sanción)** and, within 12 months, no interest.
- The surcharge is reduced by **25%** if paid in full immediately (or under an approved
  instalment plan that meets the legal conditions).

If the AEAT sends a request first (requerimiento), penalties apply instead: typically
50%–150% of the unpaid amount, before reductions. **Never wait for the letter.**

Returns with **nothing to pay** filed late (a zero 130, the 190, 349, 390) do not carry
the surcharge but can carry a **fixed fine** (art. 198 LGT; for an informative return it is
calculated per missing data item, with a minimum). The fine is **halved** when the return is
filed late without any request from the AEAT. So: file late returns immediately, even when
the result is zero.
