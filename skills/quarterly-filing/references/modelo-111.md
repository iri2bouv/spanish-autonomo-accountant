# Modelo 111 (and 115) — IRPF you withheld from others

Last verified: 2026-10-19 against the AEAT instructions:
https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-gestiones/impuestos-tasas/pagos-cuenta/modelo-111-reten_____moniales-imputaciones-renta-autoliquidacion_/instrucciones.html

## When it applies
When **you** pay someone and must withhold IRPF from that payment:
- an employee (salary) → boxes 01–03;
- a Spanish autónomo professional who invoices you with IRPF withholding → boxes 07–09
  (general rate 15%; 7% in their first three years of activity).
Office/premises rent with withholding (19%) goes on a separate form, the **115**.

The money withheld was never yours: you keep it from the supplier's payment and pay it to the AEAT.
The obligation arises when you **pay**, so the quarter follows the payment date.

## Boxes
| Box | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 01 / 02 / 03 | Work income: number of recipients / amount paid / IRPF withheld |
| 04 / 05 / 06 | Work income in kind (rare) |
| 07 / 08 / 09 | Economic activities: number of recipients / amount paid / IRPF withheld |
| 10–27 | In kind, prizes, forestry, image rights (rare for a freelancer) |
| 28 | Sum of all withholdings |
| 29 | Only in a complementary return |
| 30 | **Result to pay** |

## Quarters with no withholding
The AEAT instructions say **not** to file a 111 with all boxes empty. If you stopped
paying people with withholding for good, update your census registration (modelo 036/037)
so the AEAT stops expecting the form; otherwise you may receive a reminder letter.

## Annual summary
Every 111 filed in the year is summarised in the **modelo 190** (January). Its total must
equal the sum of the four 111s. The 115 → **modelo 180**.

## Deadlines
Q1–Q3: 1–20 April / July / October. Q4: 1–20 January (earlier than the 130's 30 January).
